JevIt: AI mail triage

Data sent to TypeSafe

To classify an email, JevIt sends its sender, recipients, subject and text (up to 6000 characters), your address, and your recipes with their learned examples to TypeSafe (api.typesafe.ai), using your own API key. This only happens for mail you triage: new mail if you turn on automatic triage, or mail you pick. Nothing is sent to the JevIt author. JevIt is not affiliated with TypeSafe.

Nothing is sent until you tick this. Untick at any time to stop all requests.

Recipes

Each recipe is one yes/no question to Jev. Refer to the mail as `email` and to yourself as `me`. Write the exact condition; Jev reads it literally. A recipe matches when the yes-probability reaches its threshold. A match always adds the recipe's tag; the action can do one more thing.

Backup file with recipes and learned examples. The API key is not included.
