Help

Connection

To classify an email, JevIt sends its sender, recipients, subject and text (up to the text limit below), its authentication results and unsubscribe link, your address, and your recipes with their learned examples to TypeSafe (api.typesafe.ai), using your own API key. This only happens for mail you triage: new mail if automatic triage is on, or mail you pick. Nothing is sent to the JevIt author. JevIt is not affiliated with TypeSafe.

Nothing is sent until you tick this. Untick at any time to stop all requests.

Usage & budget

All time
Estimated from the token counts Jev returns. Your TypeSafe bill is what counts.

Recipes

Each recipe is one yes/no question to Jev. Refer to the mail as `email` and to yourself as `me`. Write the exact condition; Jev reads it literally. A recipe matches when the yes-probability reaches its threshold. A match always adds the recipe's tag; the action can do one more thing.

Export saves recipes and learned examples to a backup file. The API key is not included.
